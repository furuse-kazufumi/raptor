## Key

Exact probability = 3/5. (P(7 before 5) = P(7)/(P(7)+P(5)) = (6/36)/((6+4)/36) = 6/10 = 3/5.) A correct answer must give exactly this reduced fraction.

## Answer

## Reasoning

**Step 1 — Probabilities per roll.** With two fair dice (36 equally likely outcomes):

- Sum = 7 occurs on {1-6, 2-5, 3-4, 4-3, 5-2, 6-1} = **6 outcomes** → P(7) = 6/36 = 1/6
- Sum = 5 occurs on {1-4, 2-3, 3-2, 4-1} = **4 outcomes** → P(5) = 4/36 = 1/9

**Step 2 — Reduce to the relevant events.** Every roll is independent and identically distributed. On any given roll exactly one of three things matters:
- "7" happens (prob 6/36),
- "5" happens (prob 4/36),
- neither happens (prob 26/36) → we simply roll again, unchanged.

The rolls where "neither" happens don't affect *which* target comes first; they only delay the decision. So the contest reduces to: given that the roll is either a 7 or a 5, what fraction of that conditional probability is the 7?

**Step 3 — Conditional probability (first "decisive" roll).**

$$
P(7 \text{ before } 5) = \frac{P(7)}{P(7) + P(5)} = \frac{6/36}{6/36 + 4/36} = \frac{6}{10} = \frac{3}{5}.
$$

**Verification via infinite series.** Let q = 26/36 be the "neither" probability. Summing over the roll on which the decision first occurs:

$$
P = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} q^{k}\cdot\frac{6}{36} = \frac{6/36}{1-26/36} = \frac{6/36}{10/36} = \frac{6}{10} = \frac{3}{5}.
$$

Both methods agree.

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{P(\text{7 before 5}) = \dfrac{3}{5}} = 0.6
$$
