## Answer

**Answer: 763**

## Method (CRT via mod 8 and mod 125)

Since 1000 = 8 × 125 with gcd(8, 125) = 1, I compute 3^777 mod 8 and mod 125 separately, then combine.

**Mod 8:**
3² = 9 ≡ 1 (mod 8), so 3^777 = 3^(2·388+1) = (3²)^388 · 3 ≡ 1 · 3 ≡ **3 (mod 8)**.

**Mod 125:**
φ(125) = 100 and gcd(3,125)=1, so by Euler 3^100 ≡ 1 (mod 125).
777 mod 100 = 77, so I need 3^77 mod 125.

Repeated squaring (mod 125):
- 3² = 9
- 3⁵ = 243 ≡ −7
- 3¹⁰ = (−7)² = 49
- 3²⁰ = 49² = 2401 ≡ 26
- 3⁴⁰ = 26² = 676 ≡ 51

Since 77 = 40 + 20 + 10 + 5 + 2:
3^77 ≡ 51 · 26 · 49 · (−7) · 9 (mod 125)
- 51 · 26 = 1326 ≡ 76
- 76 · 49 = 3724 ≡ 99
- 99 · (−7) = −693 ≡ 57
- 57 · 9 = 513 ≡ **13 (mod 125)**

**Combine with CRT:**
Solve x ≡ 3 (mod 8), x ≡ 13 (mod 125).
Write x = 125k + 13. Mod 8: 125 ≡ 5, 13 ≡ 5, so 5k + 5 ≡ 3 → 5k ≡ 6 (mod 8).
Inverse of 5 mod 8 is 5 (5·5=25≡1), so k ≡ 5·6 = 30 ≡ 6 (mod 8).
Take k = 6: x = 125·6 + 13 = **763**.

**Check:** 763 mod 8 = 3 ✓, 763 mod 125 = 13 ✓.
